# NGL Transition Admission Review — $150 Pilot

## The offer

A bounded review of **one consequential AI/automation workflow** to determine what must be true before that workflow is allowed to cause a consequential state change.

This is not generic AI governance consulting. The unit of analysis is the **right to cause a consequential transition**.

## Buyer outcome

You receive a concise admission package containing:

1. **Transition map** — consequential state changes in the selected workflow.
2. **Authority model** — who/what is permitted to cause each transition.
3. **Evidence contract** — evidence required before admission.
4. **Bypass analysis** — missing controls, authority leaks, and fail-open/fail-closed gaps.
5. **Remediation priority** — the smallest changes that materially improve controlled execution.
6. **Machine-readable admission criteria** — explicit criteria that can be translated into enforcement rules.

## Pilot scope

- One AI agent or automated workflow.
- One bounded review.
- One decision memo.
- One machine-readable admission specification.
- No production deployment or operational authority is granted by the review.

## Price

**$150 fixed pilot price.**

The pilot is intentionally small: the purpose is to establish whether transition-admission analysis creates measurable value before expanding scope.

## Admission condition

Work begins only after:

`Buyer identified → problem evidenced → offer accepted → payment verified → delivery authorized`

No payment, no delivery commitment.

## What we need from the buyer

A description or accessible representation of one workflow, its consequential actions, the intended operating boundary, and the evidence available to evaluate those actions.

## Acceptance test

The pilot succeeds when the buyer receives an actionable answer to:

> **What state transitions can this system cause, what authority should admit them, what evidence must precede them, and where can the boundary currently be bypassed?**

## Important boundary

This is an engineering/governance review, not legal advice, a certification, or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.

## $0→$1 institutional experiment

NGL is operating under a strict economic invariant:

> **NGL MUST MAKE MONEY WITHOUT SPENDING MONEY.**

The first economic proof is a verified customer payment with **$0 incremental monetary operating expenditure**. Revenue may subsequently authorize additional expenditure only through explicit economic admission controls.

## Contact / next step

If you own an AI or automation workflow where an incorrect autonomous state change would matter, propose **one workflow** for the $150 pilot.

The initial engagement is deliberately bounded. The goal is not to sell a large transformation program; it is to prove one economically valuable transition-admission decision.
